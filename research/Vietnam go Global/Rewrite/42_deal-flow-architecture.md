# Deal Flow Architecture Is The First Moat

Stay Hungry. Don't Be Foolish.

The question is not "Where can we find more Vietnamese Studios?" The better question is "How do we design sourcing, qualification, prioritization, and conversion so the best Studios do not disappear inside a noisy pipeline?"

Vietnam is not a contact-list problem. It is a Deal Flow architecture problem. You can meet many Studios and still fail if you cannot qualify them, attend many events and still fail if follow-up is weak.

Deal Flow Architecture

Four-Stage Funnel - Source -> Qualify -> Prioritize -> Convert. Each stage needs rules. Without rules, strong Studios wait too long, weak leads consume senior attention, and good opportunities are lost because nobody owns the next step.

Studio intake scorecard - eight fields - Genre fit, Team maturity, Playable quality, Data readiness, Communication reliability, Iteration speed, Monetization understanding, Trust risk.

Three SLAs that matter - First response time, Review time, Pilot decision time. The SLA matters because trust starts before the first deal.

Deal Flow is not luck. It is infrastructure.

A weak deck does not always mean a weak team. A confident pitch does not always mean a strong team. The system must see beyond surface signals. No lead moves forward without a written reason - not a feeling, not "looks good."

Sourcing channels should diversify - Founder referrals, event-qualified leads, game jam signals, LinkedIn observation, Portfolio company referrals, local advisor screening, public launch tracking. Each channel has different bias, so sourcing cannot stand alone - it must connect to qualification.

For Publishers, the job is to reduce false volume and make every next step owned.

For Studios, the job is to become easier to qualify - clearer playable, clearer Data, clearer communication.

For local operators, the opportunity is to turn market access into trusted filtering.

Action this week - Create one Studio intake scorecard and define three SLAs - first response, review time, and pilot decision time.
