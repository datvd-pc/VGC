# Repeat Business Is The Moat

Stay Hungry. Don't Be Foolish.

The question is not "How do we close the first deal?" The better question is "How do we make the second and third collaboration easier, faster, and more valuable than the first?"

The first deal proves access. The second deal proves trust. The third deal starts to look like a system.

Repeat Business

Four repeat metrics -

1. Second-deal rate - how many Studios move from first deal to second discussion.
2. Referral Rate - Do Studios refer other Studios to this Publisher?
3. Churn Reason - Why do Studios leave?
4. Repeat Partner Revenue - How much revenue comes from repeat partners?

Repeat contract is not the same as Repeat Business quality. A Studio may return only because it has no better option. A Publisher may continue only because switching cost is high. That is dependency, not a moat.

Healthy Repeat Business should show - clearer scope, faster alignment, fewer misunderstandings, better Data setup, stronger feedback quality, lower coordination cost, higher confidence in the next decision.

In Vietnam, strong Studios are often private. They may not announce every partnership or market themselves aggressively. But they talk inside trusted circles. If a Publisher treats teams well, that reputation travels quietly. If a Publisher is slow, vague, or careless with feedback, that also travels quietly.

The first deal proves access. The second deal proves trust. The third deal starts to look like a moat.

Track four numbers for all Vietnam partners - second-deal rate, referral rate, churn reason, repeat partner revenue. Then compare first deal and second deal - was the second discussion faster? Was scope clearer? Was trust higher? Was support cost lower?

If the second deal is harder than the first, the first deal probably did not create enough trust.

For Publishers, repeat business proves the operating system is becoming easier to use.

For Studios, repeat business proves the relationship created trust, not only revenue.

For ecosystem operators, referrals and second deals are cleaner signals than announcements.

Action this week - Track second-deal rate, referral rate, churn reason, and repeat partner revenue for every Vietnam partner.
