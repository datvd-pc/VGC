# Why A Hit Does Not Automatically Create A Global Studio

A hit can change a Founder's life. It does not automatically create a global company.

In Vietnam, a hit can be life-changing because the local cost base is lower. Once that happens, the Studio's next path is no longer guaranteed. Some Founders cash out. Some become lifestyle Studios. Some hunt the next hit. Very few decide to become Operators.

A hit creates money and signal. It does not create - a Data team, a Live Ops culture, a legal structure, IP rights discipline, a repeatable learning system, leadership depth, or a real operating identity.

Hit = Cash + Reputation
Operator = Cash + Reinvestment + Leadership + System + Pain Tolerance

A Publisher should ask - Does this Founder want to cash out? Is this a lifestyle Studio now? Does the team want to repeat-hit hunt, or build a real company? The answer changes the deal.

After a hit, teams often either repeat the same thing too quickly or move on without documenting what worked. Both are costly. A hit should trigger a learning review - What was the hook? What was the Retention reason? What was the Monetization moment? What part was repeatable? What part was luck?

A hit can hide weak systems - strong market moments can hide weak planning, lucky creatives can hide weak iteration, short-term revenue can hide weak management depth.

What can this team do now that it could not do before?

If the answer is only "we have more money," the company has not yet grown enough.
