# How To Build A Studio Nurture Program

Stay Hungry. Don't Be Foolish.

The question is not "How do we support Vietnamese Studios?" The better question is "How do we build a short nurture program that improves Studio capability without creating dependency or wasting Publisher time?"

"Support Studios" is too vague. Without structure, nurture becomes emotional labor that depends on individual goodwill. A Publisher that wants to build a real pipeline needs a program - short, structured, measurable, useful.

Studio Nurture Program

Six-week program -

- Week 1 - Diagnosis - Review the Studio, team, shipped work, playable quality, communication, and business goal. Output is a diagnosis, not advice.
- Week 2 - Brief - Give a narrow, specific, testable problem. Not a dream project.
- Week 3 - Playable - Studio delivers a playable build. Observe speed, clarity, technical reliability, and whether the team understood the brief.
- Week 4 - Data - Studio instruments basic events and explains what it expects to learn. This separates builders from Operators.
- Week 5 - Review - Publisher reviews playable, Data, creative direction, communication. Feedback must be written.
- Week 6 - Next Bet - Both sides decide - Pilot, Continue Nurture, Not Now, or Strategic follow-up later.

Entry criteria - at least one strong signal (fast delivery, strong craft, good Founder learning speed, clear genre fit, reliable communication, promising playable). If no signal, say Not Now respectfully.

Define what the Publisher will not provide - no unlimited Game Design consulting, no full production management, no guaranteed publishing deal, no free UA without qualification.

Nurture is not support unless it changes the next decision

Every team entering must know - why it entered, what must improve, what will be reviewed, when the decision happens, and what happens after each outcome. Clear endings create trust. Without a graduation gate, nurture becomes a soft trap where politeness damages the long-term process.

For Publishers, nurture should protect senior time while improving pipeline quality.

For Studios, nurture should provide a real learning path, not endless polite feedback.

For ecosystem operators, a short program can turn scattered talent into qualified market signal.

Action this week - Design one six-week nurture track with entry criteria, review outputs, and a clear graduation gate.
