# 25 - If I Were Interviewing A Vietnamese PM

## Goal
Create a LinkedIn infographic that helps foreign Publishers, Studio founders, and hiring managers interview Vietnamese PMs with sharper criteria.

## Viewer Benefit
The viewer should get a quick PM maturity checklist. It should help them separate task coordinators from real operators who can control scope, surface risk, align teams, and protect business outcomes.

## Core Message
A good PM is not the person who says “yes” fastest. A good PM is the person who makes reality visible before the project becomes expensive.

Interview for evidence, not adjectives.

## Data Points To Include
- 5 maturity bars: Clarity, Priority, Escalation, Risk Control, Written Follow-Up.
- 2 scenario tests: Publisher wants more scope; Dev says the build is unstable; deadline is close.
- 3 proof signals: written summary, trade-off framing, owner/deadline clarity.
- 2022-2026 context: remote cross-border production exposed weak PM communication quickly.
- 2026-2029 implication: Vietnam needs PMs who can translate between talent, Data, product, and business.
