# 14 - The Five Maturity Levels Of Vietnamese Studios

## Goal
Create a LinkedIn infographic that maps Vietnamese game Studios across five maturity levels for global collaboration.

## Viewer Benefit
The viewer should get a simple evaluation model. Foreign Publishers can classify a Studio quickly. Vietnamese founders can identify the next capability they must build to move up the ladder.

## Core Message
Not every Studio should be evaluated with the same expectations. A production vendor, prototype team, product team, operating partner, and strategic partner need different responsibilities and different deal structures.

Maturity is not pride. It is evidence.

## Data Points To Include
- 5 maturity levels: Production Vendor, Prototype Team, Product Team, Operating Partner, Strategic Partner.
- 5 upgrade gates: Scope Ownership, Data Readiness, Market Feedback, Live Ops, Business Strategy.
- 3 mismatch risks: too much responsibility too early, too little learning access, wrong contract for maturity level.
- 2026-2029 prediction: Vietnam will grow faster if Publishers design clear maturity ladders.
