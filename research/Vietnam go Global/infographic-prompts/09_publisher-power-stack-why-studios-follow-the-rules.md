# 09 - Publisher Power Stack: Why Studios Follow The Rules

## Goal
Create a LinkedIn infographic that explains why strong Publishers have structural power over small Studios in the Puzzle and Hybrid Puzzle ecosystem.

## Viewer Benefit
The viewer should see the real power stack behind publishing decisions: not only money, but legal, Data, IP rights, traffic access, creative testing, monetization, and portfolio learning.

## Core Message
Studios do not follow Publishers only because Publishers have budget. They follow because Publishers control scarce infrastructure.

In a crowded market, the winner often controls the system of discovery, measurement, risk, and scale.

## Data Points To Include
- 7 Publisher power layers: Capital, Traffic, Big Data, Creative Testing, Monetization Stack, Legal/IP Rights, Portfolio Learning.
- Studio dependency: access to UA budget, benchmarks, launch process, market feedback, and contract structure.
- 2022-2026 context: privacy shifts and rising CPI increased the value of first-party Data and portfolio benchmarks.
- 2026-2029 prediction: Publishers with stronger Data, legal structure, and distribution systems will keep shaping Studio behavior.
- Key contrast: a Studio owns production capacity; a Publisher owns market navigation.
