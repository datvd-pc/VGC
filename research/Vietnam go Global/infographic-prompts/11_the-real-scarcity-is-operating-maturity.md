# 11 - The Real Scarcity Is Operating Maturity

## Goal
Create a LinkedIn infographic that reframes Vietnam’s game market challenge: the scarce resource is not raw talent, but operating maturity.

## Viewer Benefit
The viewer should see why many Vietnamese teams can build, but fewer can operate globally. Publishers get a clearer lens for team evaluation. Vietnamese Studios get a roadmap for capability building.

## Core Message
Vietnam has talent. The harder question is whether the team can turn talent into repeatable global execution.

Operating maturity means scope control, Data discipline, written decisions, Live Ops thinking, production reliability, and business awareness.

## Data Points To Include
- 6 maturity signals: Scope Control, Data Discipline, Written Decisions, Production Reliability, Live Ops Thinking, Business Awareness.
- 4 weak signals: fast prototype, cheap cost, enthusiasm, isolated technical skill.
- 2022-2026 context: global competition made speed less valuable without learning.
- Vietnam context: many teams are early-stage, practical, and cost-aware, but often lack exposure to Publisher-level operating systems.
- 2026-2029 prediction: Vietnam’s advantage will depend on operators who can translate local talent into global process.

Use a retro arcade diagnostic screen. Put six maturity bars in the center and weak signals in a small side box. Use max four icons: gauge, chart, note, calendar.
