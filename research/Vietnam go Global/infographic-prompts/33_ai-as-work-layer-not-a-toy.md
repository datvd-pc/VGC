# 33 - AI As Work Layer, Not A Toy

## Goal
Create a LinkedIn infographic that shows Vietnamese game teams how to use AI as an operating layer in daily work, not as a novelty or content toy.

## Viewer Benefit
The viewer should get practical AI use cases for PMs, Designers, founders, and team leads: clarify goals, surface questions, compare options, generate edge cases, and store lessons.

## Core Message
AI is useful when it reduces ambiguity, speeds preparation, and improves decision quality. It is dangerous when it produces confident noise that nobody checks.

AI should sit before, during, and after work, not only at the final output stage.

## Data Points To Include
- 3 work moments: Before Work, During Work, After Work.
- 5 AI routines: Clarify Goal, Surface Questions, Compare Options, Generate Edge Cases, Store Lessons.
- 4 roles: PM, Game Designer, Developer, Founder.
- 3 risks: hallucinated facts, vague prompts, output without review.
- 2026-2029 implication: AI-literate teams will learn faster, but judgment remains the bottleneck.
