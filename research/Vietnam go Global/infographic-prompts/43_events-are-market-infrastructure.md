# Infographic Prompt - Article 43

## Goal
Reframe events from branding activities into market infrastructure: signal capture, follow-up, pilots, and repeat relationships.

## Viewer Benefit
The viewer should stop measuring events by room size and start measuring conversion quality. A Publisher gets a follow-up model. A local operator gets a way to turn meetings into market memory.

## Core Message
An event is not successful when the room is full. It is successful when qualified signal becomes follow-up, pilot, and market memory.

## Data Points To Include
- 5-stage event funnel: Attendance, Qualified Lead, Follow-Up, Pilot, Repeat.
- 4 final lead statuses: Pilot Discussion, Nurture Path, Not Now, Referral.
- 4 success meters: Trust Created, Signal Captured, Opportunities Converted, Learning Stored.
- 1 rule: event ends only when follow-up is complete.
