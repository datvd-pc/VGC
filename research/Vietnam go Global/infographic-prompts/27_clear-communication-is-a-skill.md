# 27 - Clear Communication Is A Skill

## Goal
Create a LinkedIn infographic that teaches Vietnamese game talent that communication is not soft decoration. It is a control skill in cross-border production.

## Viewer Benefit
The viewer should get a meeting and decision framework they can apply immediately with foreign partners, Publishers, leads, clients, and internal teams.

## Core Message
In global work, silence creates risk. Vague agreement creates rework. Clear writing protects time, trust, and money.

Communication is not about speaking perfect English. It is about making purpose, assumptions, decisions, owners, risks, and next steps visible.

## Data Points To Include
- 6 communication fields: Purpose, Questions, Assumptions, Decisions, Owners, Risks.
- 3 meeting stages: Before, During, After.
- 1 principle: If it is not written, it is not aligned.
- Common Vietnam-West risk: “Yes” can mean heard, agreed, avoided conflict, or committed.
- 2026-2029 implication: cross-border teams will reward written clarity more than verbal confidence.
