# 13 - Why A Hit Does Not Automatically Create A Global Studio

## Goal
Create a LinkedIn infographic that explains why one successful game does not automatically turn a Vietnamese Studio into a global operator.

## Viewer Benefit
The viewer should understand the difference between hit outcome and organizational capability. Publishers can assess post-hit risk. Vietnamese founders can see what to build after success before momentum disappears.

## Core Message
A hit creates money, confidence, and attention. It does not automatically create process, Data discipline, Live Ops capability, hiring quality, leadership maturity, or repeatable strategy.

Post-hit is a dangerous moment because comfort can replace hunger.

## Data Points To Include
- 6 post-hit requirements: Data System, Live Ops, Hiring, PM Layer, Legal/IP, Strategy Discipline.
- 4 post-hit risks: lifestyle comfort, team politics, unfocused hiring, copying the hit without learning.
- Vietnam context: success can create strong personal financial comfort relative to local living costs, which may reduce urgency unless ambition is managed.
- 2026-2029 prediction: repeat winners will separate jackpot money from operating capability.
