# 40 - Career Growth From Junior To Lead

## Goal
Create a LinkedIn infographic that gives Vietnamese game talent a practical career growth ladder based on evidence, not title inflation.

## Viewer Benefit
The viewer should get a clear path for improving from Junior to Mid, Senior, and Lead by producing reliable output, communicating clearly, making better decisions, and increasing ownership.

## Core Message
Career growth is not a title upgrade. It is evidence that your work becomes more reliable, readable, reusable, and useful to the business.

The higher the level, the more your value shifts from completing tasks to improving the system around the tasks.

## Data Points To Include
- 4 levels: Junior, Mid, Senior, Lead.
- 5 evidence bars: Output, Reliability, Thinking, Communication, Ownership.
- 4 growth transitions: from task execution to workstream ownership; from answers to trade-offs; from delivery to system improvement; from personal output to team leverage.
- 1 rule: title follows evidence.
- 2026-2029 implication: AI will make raw output cheaper, so judgment and ownership matter more.
