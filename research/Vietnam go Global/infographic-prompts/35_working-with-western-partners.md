# 35 - Working With Western Partners

## Goal
Create a LinkedIn infographic that helps Vietnamese game teams communicate more clearly with Western Publishers, clients, and partners.

## Viewer Benefit
The viewer should get a simple three-verb framework they can use in every cross-border conversation: Confirm, Clarify, Propose. It should reduce misunderstanding without requiring perfect English.

## Core Message
Western partners usually do not need perfect English. They need visible thinking.

Do not only answer. Confirm what you understood, clarify what is uncertain, and propose the next practical step.

## Data Points To Include
- 3 communication verbs: Confirm, Clarify, Propose.
- 4 misunderstanding risks: scope ambiguity, hidden blocker, different definition of done, polite agreement without commitment.
- 3 proof habits: written recap, trade-off options, risk escalation.
- 1 rule: explicit beats polite.
- 2026-2029 implication: Vietnam’s global credibility will depend heavily on communication hygiene.
