# Infographic Prompt - Article 18

Article: `18_the-yes-problem.md`

## Goal
Explain why "yes" can create expensive misunderstanding in cross-border game work.

## Viewer Benefit
Help PMs, Publishers, and Vietnamese teams reduce ambiguity after meetings and protect deadlines.

## Core Message
Yes can mean heard, agreed, polite, or committed. Written confirmation turns soft agreement into real alignment.

## Data Points To Include
- 4 meanings of yes: I Heard You, I Agree, I Want To Avoid Conflict, I Commit.
- 3 alignment steps: Repeat Back, Written Decision, Risk Escalation.
- 5 confirmation fields: Summary, Owner, Deadline, Risk, Next Step.
- 1 question: yes to direction, scope, or deadline?
- 1 rule: no agreement is real until it is written clearly.
