## Goal
Show Studio selection as portfolio allocation, not personal taste.

## Viewer Benefit
The viewer should get a decision model for allocating Publisher attention. It helps them avoid vague optimism by assigning each Studio to a portfolio bucket with matching support intensity.

## Core Message
A Publisher should not ask only whether a Studio is good or bad. It should decide the Studio's portfolio role and allocate support accordingly.

- SWOT by bucket: Strength, Weakness, Opportunity, Threat.
- 1 rule: never allocate support without naming the bucket.
