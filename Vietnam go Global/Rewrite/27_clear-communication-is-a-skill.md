# Clear Communication Is A Skill

Communication is not a soft skill. It is a control skill. Many mistakes in game production happen because someone thought they had aligned, but they had only spoken.

> Communication = purpose + questions + assumptions + decisions + written confirmation

Before the meeting: why it exists, what questions need answers, what assumptions are being made, what decision is needed. During the meeting: repeat back what you heard, name the owner, name the deadline, name the risk. After the meeting: write the summary, confirm action items and owners, leave a trail others can read.

You do not need perfect English to communicate well. You need clear thinking, written structure, and the habit of confirming meaning. The real problem is: I understood part of the message, guessed the missing part, did not ask because I was afraid of looking weak, said yes because the call was moving fast, then started work based on an assumption.

Use this structure every time:

> Purpose -> Question -> Assumption -> Risk -> Decision

Real senior communication does three things: makes the decision visible, makes the risk visible, and makes the next step visible. The strongest communicators compress confusion into something the team can act on.

As teams become more distributed and AI increases production speed, communication will become a stronger filter. Clear communication is learnable, and in a global market, learnable skills that create trust are extremely valuable.
