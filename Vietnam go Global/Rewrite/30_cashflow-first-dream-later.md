# Cashflow First, Dream Later

Cashflow is not the enemy. Confused ambition is.

If your Studio does not yet have a hit, you need runway. And runway comes from Cashflow.

Survival paths include outsourcing, co-development, prototype fees, reskin/polish, small self-publishing, and service plus R&D. There is nothing wrong with the survival layer. The mistake is when survival becomes the identity.

Cashflow -> Runway -> Capability Purchase -> Better Odds

The best use of Cashflow is not comfort. It is capability purchase - analytics, a stronger PM, a better review process, Live Ops support, legal clarity, more testing time, more learning time.

Divide Cashflow into three buckets -

1. Survival Money - Salary, rent, tools, basic runway.
2. Capability Money - Data, AI pipeline, PM training, legal review, reusable tech.
3. Option Money - Prototypes, market tests, Publisher trips, hiring experiments.

Without bucket two and three, the company may survive but not evolve.

Cashflow can become either a runway or a comfort zone. Runway means - We use money to learn, improve the pipeline, hire missing capability, build Data discipline, test better ideas, negotiate better deals. Comfort zone means - We repeat what pays, avoid harder projects, do not build management, do not document, call ourselves global but operate locally.

What does this revenue let us do that we could not do last quarter?

If the answer is nothing, you are probably spending too safely.

For individuals, the same principle applies. Do not only upgrade lifestyle. Buy capability - better English, Data analysis basics, AI workflow, design theory, analytics tools, writing ability, leadership training.

Cashflow discipline will separate Studios that survive from Studios that merely stay busy. Dream later is not defeat. It is sequencing.

Action this week - Split your current revenue into Survival Money, Capability Money, and Option Money. If everything is Survival Money, be honest about that.
