# What To Control, What To Trust

Working with a Vietnamese team is not about micro-managing everything. It is about choosing the right control points.

If you control too much, you slow the team and make them passive. If you trust too much, you lose money, time, and clarity.

> Control = scope + milestone + Data + IP + risk log
> Trust = execution style + local flow

**What to control:** Scope, Milestone, Data event map, IP and source access, Risk log, Decision gate, Delivery format.

**What to trust:** Execution style, Local hiring, Internal task flow, Production rhythm details, Creative iteration specifics.

If I were a Publisher, I would define the gates: what is done, who reviews, what Data must exist, what the kill criteria are, and what rights belong to whom. If I were a Studio, I would protect execution freedom inside that clarity.

> Control what can break the business; trust what local speed can improve.

Trust without control is naive. Control without trust is expensive. The best partnerships will be clearest about what matters and flexible about how the work is done.
