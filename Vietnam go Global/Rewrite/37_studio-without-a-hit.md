# Studio Without A Hit

If you do not have a hit yet, your first job is survival without killing learning.

> No-hit Studio = Cashflow discipline + learning discipline + kill discipline

Survival paths include outsourcing, co-development, prototype fees, reskin/polish, small self-publishing, and service plus R&D. They are not weak paths. They are the survival layer. The mistake is confusing movement with progress.

Each path needs a different Operating System and different metrics:

- **Cashflow Studio** - margin, delivery reliability, repeat clients, runway.
- **Prototype Hunter** - number of tests, speed to playable, learning per test, Publisher access.
- **Co-Development** - trust, code quality, documentation, production predictability, team stability.
- **Talent Farm** - training speed, promotion quality, retention of strong people, reputation.
- **Original IP** - creative ownership, audience understanding, funding path, long-term operating capability.

If I were a Vietnamese Founder without a hit, I would write one page: what business model we are choosing for the next 12 months, what cash runway supports it, what capability gap blocks the next level, what kind of Publisher or Client fits us, and what kind of project we should refuse.

> Strategy is not only what you choose. It is also what you stop accepting.

A Studio can survive while still building capability. A Studio can be profitable while still improving its odds. A Studio can stay small while still becoming harder to replace.

The question is not whether the Studio has a hit today. The question is whether the Studio is becoming more valuable every quarter.

The hit may come later. The Operating Maturity must be built now.
