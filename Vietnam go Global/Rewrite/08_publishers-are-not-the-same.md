# Publishers Are Not The Same

Many Studios pitch every Publisher as if they were the same buyer. They are not. That mistake is expensive.

Voodoo, SayGames, Supersonic, Rollic, Homa, TapNation, CrazyLabs, and Lion/AppLovin all sit inside mobile publishing, but their strategies, risk models, and selection logic differ significantly.

- Voodoo leans toward industrialized filtering and high-volume prototype testing.
- SayGames leans toward fewer bets and deeper partnerships.
- Supersonic leans toward transparency, Data, and Unity-integrated publishing.
- Rollic leans toward Hybrid Puzzle craftsmanship and deeper long-term titles.
- Homa leans toward tool-assisted hybridization and Portfolio expansion.
- TapNation leans toward broad Studio networks and infrastructure-heavy scaling.
- CrazyLabs leans toward broad funnel Monetization and the long tail.
- Lion/AppLovin leans toward platform-backed publishing with a strong growth stack.

The real formula is not "Good Game + Good Publisher = Success." It is -

Game Signal + Team Maturity + Publisher Bottleneck = Fit

Foreign Publishers are buying optionality, not just prototypes. The wrong fit wastes time on both sides.

A Vietnamese Studio should never ask only "Which Publisher is biggest?" Better questions - Which Publisher's bottleneck matches our strength? Which needs our kind of output? Which can help us grow? Which will keep learning with us?

Different Publishers also require different internal work. A volume-driven Publisher needs speed and fast feedback absorption. A partnership-driven Publisher needs clear communication and written records. A platform-backed Publisher needs tool fluency. A craftsmanship-led Publisher needs deeper Level Design and Retention thinking.

In the next 2-4 years, Publisher differentiation will matter more as AI makes raw production easier. The real value will move toward the Publisher's Operating Model.

The question is not which Publisher is best. The question is which one matches your current team.
