# How To Build A Vietnam Operating Pod

You do not need a perfect Vietnamese Studio. You need the right operating pod.

A good pod contains - one local PM, one senior dev lead, one game Designer or economy thinker, one analytics layer, one creative bridge, and one weekly decision cadence.

Operating Pod = Local PM + Senior Dev Lead + Designer/Economy + Analytics + Creative Bridge + Weekly Cadence

This gives the Publisher clarity without destroying local speed. It gives the Vietnamese team clear responsibility structure. A 6-8 week pod tests seriousness without pretending the system is bigger than it is.

The pod answers - Can the team understand the player promise? Turn a brief into a focused playable? Cut scope intelligently? Set up basic events? Explain risk early? Write clear English updates? Learn from one test and improve the next?

Weekly cadence - Monday - agree hypothesis, scope, risk, owner, decision needed. Wednesday - review playable progress, blockers, unclear decisions. Friday - lock what changed, what was learned, and next gate preparation.

AI sits inside the pod as a work layer - summarize meetings, translate feedback into decision questions, draft risk logs, create QA checklists, clean up English updates. Bad use - replacing product judgment or hiding unclear thinking behind polished language.

Role ownership - Dev Lead owns technical risk. Designer owns player behavior and feature priority. PM owns clarity, cadence, documentation, escalation. Publisher PM owns direction, test logic, feedback quality.

The point is not to make the team smaller. The point is to make the collaboration clearer.
