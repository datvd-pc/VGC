# The Cashflow-First Path Of A Vietnamese Game Studio

Most Vietnamese Studios do not start with an IP dream. They start with payroll.

A Studio with a short runway will not think like a company with deep capital. This is why "just be ambitious" is weak advice. The better question is: what Cashflow path can keep the team alive while still letting it learn?

Realistic paths include outsourcing, co-development, prototype fees, reskin/polish, small self-publishing, or a hybrid service + R&D mix. None are shameful. They are the survival layer. The problem is when survival becomes the whole strategy.

> Survival Cashflow -> small learning budget -> capability purchase -> better odds

The right use of Cashflow is not comfort. It is buying a better future version of the team: better analytics, a stronger PM, a real economy Designer, Live Ops support, legal clarity, time to test, and time to kill bad ideas early.

Cashflow shapes culture. A Studio that survives on fast, practical Cashflow learns to respect constraints. A Studio that survives only on hope learns to ignore them.

For foreign Publishers, this explains behavior. Sometimes a Vietnamese team is not being difficult. It is protecting its runway. If you read that as lack of ambition, you will misread the team. If you read it as rational constraint management, you can partner better.

> Cashflow is runway. Runway buys learning. Learning buys leverage.

The cleanest question is not "is the Cashflow big or small?" but "does this buy a better next quarter?"
