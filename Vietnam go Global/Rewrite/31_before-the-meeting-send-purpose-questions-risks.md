# Before The Meeting, Send Purpose, Questions, Risks

Good meetings start before the meeting. Too many meetings begin with no agenda, no clear decision needed, and no visible risk.

> Meeting prep = purpose + questions + risks + decision needed

Send this in advance:

> Purpose
> Current Context
> Questions
> Assumptions
> Risks
> Decisions Needed

Example:

> Purpose: align on the next playable build.
> Questions: should we optimize for first-session clarity or add meta progression now?
> Assumption: the next test is mainly for D1 and first-session comprehension.
> Risks: adding meta now may delay the build by 3-4 days.
> Decisions Needed: cut meta for this build, or delay test to include it.

This does three things: shows ownership (you are not waiting passively), reduces misunderstanding (wrong assumptions can be corrected before wasting time), and improves power balance (clear preparation makes you look like an Operator, not only a vendor).

Many Vietnamese teams enter meetings in listening mode, which can be read as passivity in cross-border settings. A strong team prepares the room before the room starts.

Short notes get answers. Long notes often get ignored. You are not only writing - you are designing the response you want.

AI can help draft the note from rough context, then edit it yourself. The human responsibility is judgment: is the risk real, the question specific, the decision needed now, the tone direct but respectful, and the note short enough to read?

Preparation is respect. Preparation is also risk control.
