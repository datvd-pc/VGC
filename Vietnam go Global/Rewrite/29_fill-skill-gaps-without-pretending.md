# Fill Skill Gaps Without Pretending

Everyone has skill gaps. The real difference is whether they know how to fill them.

Learn = Read -> Note -> Compare -> Test -> Reflect -> Update Playbook

The problem is that many people try to hide gaps instead of building a system to close them.

Common gaps for young Vietnamese game talent -

AI Gap - Most people use AI as search boxes or idea generators. Better use - turn notes into structured decisions, compare competitor flows, draft edge-case checklists, summarize reports, rewrite unclear English updates. AI should improve your thinking process, not replace it.

Pipeline Gap - Every project starts from scratch - different naming, improvised event maps, inconsistent postmortems. A better pipeline includes prototype templates, event naming conventions, build/QA/level review checklists, meeting note formats, and postmortem templates.

Insight Gap - Many juniors can describe what they built but not why it matters. Insight means connecting work to behavior - why would the player understand this mechanic? Why would they return on day 7? Why would this economy create pressure without frustration?

Communication Gap - Ask questions before starting, write assumptions, confirm decisions, expose risks early, summarize after meetings.

Positioning Gap - Know what role you actually play. A Developer may need to become a technical partner. A Designer may need to become a product thinker. A PM may need to become a decision Operator.

Pick one gap for 30 days. Build proof - one better template, one clearer document, one better postmortem, one AI-assisted workflow. Then show the proof.

If the gap is AI, use AI to clean meeting notes and turn them into better recaps.

If the gap is Data, learn one metric deeply and connect it to one real decision.

If the gap is Communication, write a pre-meeting brief before the next important call and compare the alignment after.

The people who grow fastest close one important gap at a time and make that improvement visible.
