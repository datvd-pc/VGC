# How To Attract High-Quality Studios

Stay Hungry. Don't Be Foolish.

The question is not "How do we convince more good Studios to work with us?" The better question is "What must our Publisher promise be so a strong Vietnamese Studio sees us as an upgrade, not just another buyer?"

Strong Studios do not choose partners only by check size. They choose by leverage: Will this Publisher make our next decision better? Will the relationship improve our Data, creative process, Monetization thinking, Live Ops discipline, legal clarity, or market access? Will we learn something that stays after the deal?

**Studio Value Proposition.**

Five attraction factors:

1. **Fairness** - clear, honest terms.
2. **Speed** - fast review and response.
3. **Useful feedback** - feedback that improves decision quality.
4. **Learning access** - Data visibility, creative insights, postmortems.
5. **Deal clarity** - explicit IP, Data, and commercial terms.

A Publisher is attractive when a strong Studio can explain the next step without guessing. After two meetings, the Studio should know: what the Publisher wants, what standard the game must pass, what Data matters, what support is real, what timeline applies, and what would make the Publisher say no.

Write the partner promise explicitly: who you work with, what you provide, what you do not provide, how submissions are reviewed, how fast Studios receive response, what happens after rejection, what happens after a successful pilot, what a long-term partner can expect.

Clear boundaries are part of the value proposition. Overpromising support creates disappointment. In Vietnam, disappointment is not always expressed directly - the Studio may become slower, avoid hard conversations, or say yes without fully committing.

> Good Studios do not only ask who can publish them. They ask who can make their next decision better.

Attraction begins before negotiation. The first email, first call, first review note, and first rejection already tell the market what kind of partner you are. Strong Studios notice process quality early.
