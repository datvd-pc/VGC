# AI As A Work Layer, Not A Toy

AI is useful when it reduces ambiguity. It is not useful when it only makes work look faster.

AI Layer = Input Cleaner + Thought Organizer + Memory Assistant

Good uses - Meeting agenda and summary, brief scan, question generation, comparing options, learning plans, risk lists, first drafts of emails or recaps, comparing mechanics, summarizing player feedback, spotting patterns in fail Data, preparing Publisher updates.

Bad uses blindly - Design judgment, final business decisions, product taste, anything that needs direct ownership.

The practical test

Did AI help you make a better decision, or did it only help you create more text?

For a Junior Game Designer - write the level hypothesis, ask AI to challenge it from player behavior, check against clarity/fairness/near-miss/retry motivation, organize Data patterns after results, then rewrite the lesson in your own words.

For a PM - ask AI to turn rough meeting notes into decisions, owners, and risks. Remove anything AI invented. Add missing context. Send a short recap.

For a Founder - use AI to compare scenarios - keep current Cashflow model, invest in Data capability, hire a Senior PM, pitch Publishers, build original IP, reduce team size.

The risk is that AI makes weak thinking look fluent. Many people will write better updates and better pitch decks. But experienced Publishers will still see shallow thinking. The advantage will belong to people who use AI to build better judgment, cleaner systems, and faster learning loops - not just more polished output.

AI should sit in specific moments of the work - before work (clarify goal, surface missing questions), during work (compare options, summarize notes, generate edge cases), after work (check what AI got wrong, store the lesson, reuse the pattern).
