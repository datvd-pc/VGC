# The Five Maturity Levels Of Vietnamese Studios

Do not ask whether a Vietnamese Studio is good. Ask what maturity level it is operating at.

I group Vietnamese Studios into five levels:

1. **Production Vendor** - strong at execution, speed, and cost.
2. **Prototype Hunter** - strong at market search and Creative Testing.
3. **Reskin / Polish Cashflow Studio** - strong at survival and Cashflow.
4. **Emerging Hybrid Operator** - starting to own Data, Monetization, and Retention.
5. **Mature Global Operator** - can hold learning, IP, and long-term value.

Each level optimizes differently: speed/reliability, signal discovery, survival, learning/Operating Control, and long-term value respectively.

> Fit = maturity level + scope + control model

A team can be excellent for co-dev and risky for Live Ops. A team can be perfect for prototype production and weak for Monetization architecture. The same Studio can be right for one Publisher and wrong for another.

If I were a Publisher, I would design relationships by level: Level 1 gets controlled scope with tight milestones; Level 2 gets fast tests with clear kill criteria; Level 3 gets Cashflow-friendly collaboration; Level 4 gets capability building and Data sharing; Level 5 gets strategic partnership.

If I were a Studio, I would stop pretending to be a level I am not. That only creates bad contracts and bad expectations.

Level 1 and 2 help you get in the room. Level 3 keeps the company alive. Level 4 and 5 help you compound.
