# Studio Selection Is Portfolio Governance

Stay Hungry. Don't Be Foolish.

The question is not "Is this Studio good or bad?" The better question is "What Portfolio role should this Studio play, and what level of support or capital does it deserve now?"

A Studio can be weak for launch but strong for nurture, strong at production but weak at product ownership, commercially small today but valuable if its learning speed is high. Selection is not only judgment. It is allocation.

**Portfolio Governance.**

Four-bucket board:

1. **Pilot** - enough signal to test a concrete opportunity. Give a clear test brief, review cadence, decision date.
2. **Nurture** - not ready for serious capital yet, but may improve with structured support. Give a clear learning path with graduation rule.
3. **Strategic Partner** - deserves deeper learning, better access, longer-term planning. Give deeper Data access and roadmap discussion.
4. **Not Now** - fit is weak, timing is wrong, or opportunity cost is too high. Reject cleanly and respectfully.

> Never allocate support without naming the bucket.

Without buckets, the Publisher either over-invests in weak leads or under-invests in future partners. Treating every positive conversation as a future deal is the most common market mistake.

Vietnamese Founders can be polite. Foreign Publishers can be optimistic. That combination creates ambiguity. Buckets turn optimism into a decision.

For each active Studio, write: current bucket, next evidence needed, support level, review cadence, owner, reason for the decision.

> Studio selection is not taste. It is Portfolio Governance.
