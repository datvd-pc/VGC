# 07 - The Puzzle Ecosystem Is Not About Making Games Anymore

## Goal
Create a premium LinkedIn infographic for foreign Publishers, Studio leaders, investors, and operators. It must show that the modern Puzzle and Hybrid Puzzle market is no longer only about making games. It is an operating ecosystem: Build, Grow, Operate, Control.

## Viewer Benefit
The viewer should understand the market map in 5 seconds. A Publisher sees where power comes from. A Studio sees why a good game alone is no longer enough. A Vietnamese operator sees the missing capability layers Vietnam must build to go global.

## Core Message
The Puzzle ecosystem moved from “make a game” to “control the system around the game.”

In 2022-2026, CPI pressure, privacy changes, UA competition, Live Ops maturity, and Data advantage pushed the market toward operating systems, not isolated prototypes.

## Data Points To Include
- Global mobile games scale: about $90B+ annual consumer spend and ads-driven revenue combined.
- Puzzle remains one of the most durable mobile categories by reach and monetization.
- Post-IDFA and rising CPI made targeting less certain and creative testing more important.
- Hybrid Puzzle blends Retention, Live Ops, Ads Monetization, IAP, and event design.
- 2026-2029 prediction: advantage shifts to teams that control Data, traffic, monetization, Live Ops, IP, and partner networks.

Main title: “Puzzle Is Not Just Game-Making Anymore”

Subtitle: “The Winner Controls The Ecosystem.”

Four power pillars:
- Build: Studio, Dev Team, Game Design, Pipeline.
- Grow: UA, Creatives, ASO, Organic Reach.
- Operate: Live Ops, Events, Analytics, Monetization.
- Control: Data, IP Rights, Legal, Distribution, Capital.

Key formula:
“Modern Puzzle Advantage = Build + Grow + Operate + Control”

Future signal:
“2026-2029: Good Games Need Operating Power.”

Bottom takeaway:

## Quality Check
