# 31 - Before The Meeting, Send Purpose, Questions, Risks

## Goal
Create a LinkedIn infographic that teaches Vietnamese game teams a concrete pre-meeting habit for working with foreign Publishers and partners.

## Viewer Benefit
The viewer should get a copyable meeting-prep structure. It should help them get better answers, reduce passive misunderstanding, and make foreign partners feel the team is serious before the call starts.

## Core Message
A good meeting starts before the meeting. If the purpose, context, questions, assumptions, risks, and decisions needed are not written early, the call becomes a guessing session.

Preparation is not bureaucracy. It is respect for everyone’s time.

## Data Points To Include
- 6 pre-meeting fields: Purpose, Current Context, Questions, Assumptions, Risks, Decisions Needed.
- Timing rule: send before the call, not after everyone is already confused.
- 3 benefits: better preparation, faster decisions, fewer hidden assumptions.
- Common cross-border issue: one side thinks the call is for approval, the other thinks it is for exploration.
- 2026-2029 implication: written prep becomes a signal of operating maturity.
