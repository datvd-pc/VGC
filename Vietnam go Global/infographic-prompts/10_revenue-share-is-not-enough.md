# 10 - Revenue Share Is Not Enough

## Goal
Create a LinkedIn infographic that challenges the common belief that revenue share after a hit is enough to motivate or develop Vietnamese Studios.

## Viewer Benefit
The viewer should understand why revenue share is valuable but incomplete. Publishers learn what else keeps Studios aligned. Studios learn what to negotiate beyond upside: learning, Data access, asset rights, credit, and capability transfer.

## Core Message
Revenue share rewards success after it happens. It does not automatically create capability before success happens.

For many Vietnamese teams, the path to a hit is long, uncertain, and expensive. They need learning systems and cashflow discipline before the jackpot.

## Data Points To Include
- 4 missing incentives: upfront cashflow, Data access, learning rights, next-deal progression.
- 5 non-money assets: postmortems, benchmarks, templates, reusable pipeline, market feedback.
- Hit probability is low in a crowded global market.
- Vietnam context: after one success, local cost of living can reduce urgency for some team members unless ambition and operating culture are built.
- 2026-2029 prediction: capability-based partnerships will beat simple revenue-share promises.
