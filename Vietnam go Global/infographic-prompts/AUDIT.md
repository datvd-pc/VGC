## Rule
If the article does not have external market data, do not force fake numbers into the infographic.
Use:
- counts;
- stages;
- levels;
- decision trees;
- score bars;
- checklists;
- before/after comparisons.

## Next Writing Pass
When expanding the article body later, add at least one of:
- a date range;
- a market size or share;
- a decision metric;
- a before/after comparison;
- a count of layers, levels, or steps.
