# Infographic Prompt - Article 15

Article: `15_small-ambition-or-rational-risk-management.md`

## Goal
Reframe cautious Vietnamese Founders as rational risk managers, not automatically low-ambition people.

## Viewer Benefit
Help foreign Publishers and Investors avoid misreading a Vietnamese Founder. Help Vietnamese Founders explain whether they are choosing safety, building ambition, or avoiding risk.

## Core Message
Small ambition is not always the issue. Runway, proof, incentives, expected upside, and pain tolerance shape Founder behavior.

## Data Points To Include
- 4 risk meters: Runway, Proof, Pain Tolerance, Expected Upside.
- 2 possible paths: Comfort Path, Build Path.
- 4 Publisher questions: What is the Founder optimizing for? What is the local cost of failure? Is the team protecting survival or avoiding growth? What would make them accept more risk?
- 3 Founder choices: Stay Small, Cashflow First, Build Global.
- 1 decision rule: judge incentives before judging ambition.
