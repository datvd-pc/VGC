# 34 - Operating Hygiene

## Goal
Create a LinkedIn infographic that makes operating hygiene feel like a serious production advantage for Vietnamese game teams.

## Viewer Benefit
The viewer should get a clear checklist of operating objects a team should maintain: decision log, risk log, postmortem, handoff, weekly review, and ownership. It should make workflow discipline feel practical, not bureaucratic.

## Core Message
Operating hygiene is team memory. Without it, teams repeat mistakes, lose context, misunderstand decisions, and depend too much on individual heroics.

Fast teams are not only fast because they move. They are fast because they remember.

## Data Points To Include
- 5 operating objects: Decision Log, Risk Log, Postmortem, Handoff, Weekly Review.
- 3 cadences: Daily Check, Weekly Review, Project Postmortem.
- 1 owner per object.
- 3 symptoms of poor hygiene: repeated mistakes, unclear decisions, slow onboarding.
- 2026-2029 implication: global partners will trust teams that can show their operating memory.
