# 32 - After The Meeting, Send Summary, Owners, Next Steps

## Goal
Create a LinkedIn infographic that teaches Vietnamese game teams how to turn meetings into written operating memory.

## Viewer Benefit
The viewer should get a practical post-meeting recap template. It helps avoid “we talked about it” confusion and gives foreign partners confidence that decisions will survive after the call.

## Core Message
A meeting without a recap is only a conversation. A recap turns conversation into execution.

In cross-border game production, memory cannot live only in someone’s head. It must live in written decisions, owners, deadlines, open questions, and risks.

## Data Points To Include
- 7 recap fields: Summary, Decisions, Owners, Deadlines, Open Questions, Risks, Next Step.
- 3 failure modes: forgotten trade-off, unclear owner, invisible blocker.
- 1 rule: send recap while the discussion is still fresh.
- 2022-2026 context: remote collaboration increased the cost of undocumented decisions.
- 2026-2029 implication: operating memory becomes a competitive advantage for Vietnamese teams.
