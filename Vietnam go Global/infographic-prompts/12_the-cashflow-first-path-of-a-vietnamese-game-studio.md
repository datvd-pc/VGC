# 12 - The Cashflow-First Path Of A Vietnamese Game Studio

## Goal
Create a LinkedIn infographic that explains why many Vietnamese game Studios act cashflow-first and how that can be either a trap or a strategic path.

## Viewer Benefit
The viewer should understand Vietnamese Studio behavior more accurately. Publishers see why teams may avoid big risky bets. Studios see how to use cashflow to build capability instead of staying small forever.

## Core Message
Cashflow-first is not automatically small ambition. In an early ecosystem with limited runway, it can be rational risk management.

But cashflow becomes a trap when it buys comfort instead of capability.

## Data Points To Include
- 4 cashflow-first reasons: limited runway, family pressure, small local cost base, low hit probability.
- 4 upgrade uses: Data system, PM layer, production pipeline, Publisher relationship.
- 3 trap signals: reskin forever, no postmortem, no senior capability built.
- 2022-2026 context: tougher UA economics made hit-chasing harder.
- 2026-2029 prediction: best Vietnamese Studios will combine cashflow discipline with capability compounding.
