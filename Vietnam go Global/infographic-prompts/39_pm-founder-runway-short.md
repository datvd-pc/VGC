# 39 - When Runway Is Short, Honesty Is Strategy

## Goal
Create a LinkedIn infographic for Vietnamese PMs and founders who must make practical decisions under short runway.

## Viewer Benefit
The viewer should get a harsh but useful filter for choosing projects, reducing waste, and communicating reality when money, time, or team energy is limited.

## Core Message
Short runway punishes vague ambition. When time is limited, every project must prove whether it pays now, teaches something useful, builds leverage, or only consumes attention.

Honesty is not pessimism. It is how the team survives long enough to improve.

## Data Points To Include
- 3 operating modes: Survival Mode, Learning Mode, Growth Mode.
- 6 runway inputs: Cash, Burn, Team Capacity, Client Pipeline, Prototype Pipeline, Decision Speed.
- 4 project filters: Pays Now, Teaches Something, Builds Leverage, Only Consumes Attention.
- 3 hard actions: cut scope, renegotiate, stop low-value work.
- 2026-2029 implication: teams with clear runway logic will outlast teams chasing noise.
